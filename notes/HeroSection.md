Hero Section

The hero section is the first element visitors see, serving as an introduction to the developer. This section should include:

1. Developer’s Name: Full name or professional alias.
2. Job Title: E.g., "Full-Stack Web Developer" or "Software Engineer."
3. Professional Photo or Avatar: A photo or avatar that reflects a professional image.
4. Brief Introduction: A short description emphasizing core skills or services, such as "Building scalable and high-performance web solutions for your business needs."
4. Call-to-Action (CTA): Button to direct visitors, such as “View Portfolio” or “Contact Me.”
